# yab - Yet another browser

A fast native macOS browser with an iPhone/Android companion. WebKit by default, Chromium on Apple silicon per tab/site, with Chrome extensions support. Built-in ad blocker, reading mode, split view, floating video, passwords in keychain, agents in their own tabs, E2E encrypted sync.

Repo: https://github.com/yetanotherbrowser/yab
Site: https://yetanotherbrowser.com
